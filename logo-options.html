Janbot — five concepts, five metaphors

not styling variants · pick the IDEA that fits · dark / native / orange rationed

01 · the colleague
“My name is Jan” badge
The classic name tag. Instantly human, warm, a little cheeky.
Says: this isn’t software, it’s someone on your team. Nobody in AI does this.
02 · signs its work
The signature
A handwritten autograph with an orange underline flourish.
Says: an agent that takes ownership and signs off on what it did for you. Personal, confident, premium.
03 · the caretaker
Janitor’s key
“Jan” is one letter from janitor — the agent with keys to your machine that tidies up and runs on schedule.
Says: trusted local access + maintenance. An ownable metaphor, not a generic AI orb.
04 · native mac
Classic-Mac pixel mark
A 1-bit, Susan-Kare-era bitmap “jan”, single orange pixel as the dot.
Says: proudly, unmistakably a Mac app. Nostalgic craft, not AI-slop gradient.
05 · extra · hero icon
Key, as the app tile
Concept 03 built into a full macOS icon with depth + a faint warm glow.
Says: the dock/menu-bar face of the caretaker idea. Distinct at any size.
